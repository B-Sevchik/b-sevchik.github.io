About

I am a PhD student in the Poldrack Lab at Stanford University, where I work with fMRI data to elucidate the brain regions involved in distinct cognitive control processes. I was previously a data analyst at PennLINC where I worked on multimodal neuroimaging projects in brain development and co-led the SYPRES initiative. I did my undergrad at Duke University, where I studied Neuroscience and Spanish and worked in the Egner Lab and Cogan Lab.
